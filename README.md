# Steps - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Steps ("the app") is a free Android game published by Haseeb, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

## 1. Information we collect
We do not collect, store or sell your personal information. The app has no accounts and no sign-up. Your best score is saved only on your own device and is never sent to us.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app shows optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. An ad is shown only if you choose to watch one, for example to continue after a game over. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads).

## 3. Permissions
The app uses internet access only so that ads can load. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

## 4. Children
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 5. Data retention and security
Because we do not collect personal data, we do not store any on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes the locally saved score from your device.

## 6. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
